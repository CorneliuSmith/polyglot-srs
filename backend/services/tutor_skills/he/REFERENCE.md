# Curriculum reference — Hebrew (he)

The app's grammar path in teaching order. These point titles are exactly what the learner sees on their cards, and weak grammar items arrive labeled with them — use the same names when coaching, and sequence new material in this order.

## A1
- Personal pronouns and grammatical gender — Choose the right pronoun for gender and number
- No verb "to be" in the present tense — Build a sentence with no linking verb
- Adjective gender agreement — Match an adjective's ending to its noun's gender
- The definite article ה־ — Say "the" in Hebrew
- Present-tense verbs change with gender and number — Conjugate a present-tense verb for its subject
- Negation with לא — Say what isn't or doesn't happen
- Existence: יש and אין — Say that something does or doesn't exist
- Possession with של — Say who something belongs to
