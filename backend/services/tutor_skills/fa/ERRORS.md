# Common learner errors — Persian

- **را omitted on a definite object, or added to an indefinite one.**
  Learners either forget را entirely (defaulting to English's lack of
  object marking) or over-apply it to generic/indefinite objects. Ask
  "does the speaker already have a specific one in mind?" before deciding.
- **Present stem used where the past stem belongs, or vice versa.** Since
  the two stems aren't predictable from each other (رفتن → می‌رو-/رفت-),
  learners guess the past stem by trying to "reverse" the present marker
  instead of memorizing it separately.
- **می‌ dropped from the ongoing present.** Learners conjugate the bare
  stem with personal endings and forget the می‌ prefix that marks the verb
  as present/durative rather than subjunctive.
- **نمی‌ split into two words.** The negative prefix attaches directly to
  the verb; learners sometimes write it as a separate "not" word the way
  English negation works.
- **Compound-verb conjugation on the wrong part.** In light-verb compounds
  (کار کردن "to work"), only کردن conjugates — learners sometimes try to
  inflect the noun/adjective half instead.
- **Ezafe lost when reading aloud.** Because it's usually unwritten,
  learners often don't realize a linking vowel belongs between a noun and
  its following adjective/possessor, and read the two words as
  unconnected.
