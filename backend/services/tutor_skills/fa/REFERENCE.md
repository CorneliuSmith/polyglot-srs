# Curriculum reference — Persian (fa)

The app's grammar path in teaching order. These point titles are exactly what the learner sees on their cards, and weak grammar items arrive labeled with them — use the same names when coaching, and sequence new material in this order.

## A1
- Personal pronouns and budan ("to be") — Say who/what something is
- Verb-final word order (SOV) — Build a basic sentence in Persian order
- Personal verb endings: -am, -i, -ad — Conjugate a present-tense verb for its subject
- Negation with na- / nemi- — Say what you don't do or don't want
- Adjectives follow the noun — Put adjectives in the right place
- Yes/no questions: same word order, just a question mark — Ask a yes/no question without reordering the sentence
- The plural suffix -ha — Make a noun plural
- Ra marks a definite direct object — Mark a direct object you already know about
