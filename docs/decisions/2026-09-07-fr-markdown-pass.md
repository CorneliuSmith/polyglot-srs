The Phase 3 closing step for French (`docs/plans/markdown-explanations.md`), the first course through it. Three writers took fourteen explanations each, against `docs/quality/fr.md` and the house style; a second reader refused anything that formatted for its own sake or changed what the text claims without saying so.

**Restraint was the hard part and it held.** Nineteen explanations came back byte-identical. The typesetter already renders term/gloss pairs and arrow derivations as tables, so markdown adds nothing there; the tables that landed are real paradigms — person by form for *être*, *avoir* and the `-er`, `-ir` and `-re` endings, article by condition by example for `le/la/les` and `un/une/des`.

These are corrections to what the explanation CLAIMS, not formatting. Each was declared by the writer and checked by the reader.

The largest is *Irregular verbs in the present*: four paradigms were missing their **vous** form entirely (`pouvez`, `voulez`, `venez`, `prenez`) and several had lost the **tu** form, so a learner met a conjugation table with holes in it. Only *aller* had been complete.
